# V16 — Safari/iPhone transition deadlock after second film

Observed failure: mobile Safari frequently does not send canplay or seeked for an offscreen 1080p clip while paused, until the app calls play(). Previous bridge waited for canplay before setting chapter 3 and attempting playback, creating permanent bridgeBusy and swallowing ALL subsequent swipes. This version switches directly from the aligned last frame of film 02 to the first-frame POSTER of 03, invokes video.play() during the user's touchend event, and never requires canplay before the transition. The second source still begins at 1.8s. All clips remain strictly gated by actual ended events; Skip Intro is unchanged.

Forward clip 03 is prewarmed while film 02 plays; three 1080p reverse assets are now *lazy*-loaded only when the user scrolls backward, so mobile Safari isn't forced to keep six video decoders/media source loads during intro. The fallback "Tap to Continue Film" is always available when playback fails, even if the onError handler recorded a recoverable network issue. QA simulates HAVE_NOTHING readyState at the second→third junction to prevent a regression. No video transcoding or degradation.
